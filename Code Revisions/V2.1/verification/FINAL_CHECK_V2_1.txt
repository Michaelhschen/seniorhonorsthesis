ECONOMIC INTERPRETATION
The 80.66% separate-pipeline and 13.89% common-classifier results are different
conditional comparisons. The profile-matched contrast reverses with two or three
states. BIC does not select four states for all panels. Near-selected nowcast fits
retain the sign but change the magnitude. The declared recession diagnostic still
misses both recession months. None of these checks establishes historical
real-time classification, a stable economic taxonomy, or portfolio performance.
